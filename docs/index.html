CYD Particle Life Web Installer

Install the Particle Life simulation directly to your ESP32-2432S028R (CYD).

Connect your CYD board via USB, choose your board variant below, and click "Install".

1. CYD2 (ST7789) [Default / Original]

CYD2 with ST7789 display panel. Default target for the original source code.

2. CYD1 (ILI9341)

CYD1 with standard ILI9341 display panel.

3. CYD1 (ILI9341 - Inverted Colors)

CYD1 with ILI9341 display panel where colors appear inverted (e.g., white background instead of black).

Touch Controls

  • Viewport Tap: Cycle through curated rule matrices (cellular clusters, chaotic orbits, crystal lattices, and predator-prey dynamics).
  • [H+] Button: Increment clock hour.
  • [M+] Button: Increment clock minute.
  • [00s] Button: Reset seconds to 00.

Hardware Specifications

  • Microcontroller: ESP32-D0WDQ6 (Dual-core 240MHz)
  • Display: 2.8" SPI TFT LCD (240x320 resolution)
  • Touch Controller: XPT2046 Resistive Touch
  • Performance: 900 Particles @ 30+ FPS (Core 0: Physics / Core 1: SPI DMA)
